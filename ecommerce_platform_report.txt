ECOMMERCE PLATFORM - UX FEATURES REPORT
=======================================
Date: 22 October 2026
Project: Oakline ecommerce platform
Frontend: Next.js 16 (App Router)
Backend: not connected yet
Admin panel: /admin (no login page yet)

PURPOSE
-------
This report lists the frontend features added or proposed to make the
shopping experience simple, clear and comfortable for customers, and to
give the store owner a practical admin area.

Status key:
[DONE]     = implemented in the frontend
[PROPOSED] = recommended for the next stage


1. GUEST CHECKOUT ONLY  [DONE]
------------------------------
- Removed the account/registration icon from the header.
- Customers can order without creating an account.
- Keeps the checkout short and reduces the steps before purchase.
- The header now shows only: language switch, search and cart.

Why it helps:
- Fewer decisions for the customer.
- No password management or account recovery needed.
- Suitable for a small store that mainly uses cash on delivery.


2. CLEANER STOREFRONT HEADER  [DONE]
------------------------------------
- Removed unused account UI.
- Kept the cart always visible with an item-count badge.
- Kept the search box and Arabic/English language switch.
- Product, shop and cart pages stay focused on shopping.

Why it helps:
- Less visual noise.
- Customers immediately understand where to search, switch language
  and open the cart.


3. ADMIN DASHBOARD  [DONE]
--------------------------
Path: /admin

- Summary cards: revenue, orders, pending orders and products.
- Revenue chart for the last six months.
- Order status breakdown with simple progress bars.
- Recent orders table with customer, phone, total and status.
- Top products list.
- Responsive layout: sidebar on desktop, compact top menu on mobile.
- No login page for now; a guest-admin state is shown instead.

Why it helps:
- The owner sees the health of the store at a glance.
- Orders that need attention are easy to spot.
- Works on both desktop and phone.


4. PRODUCT MANAGEMENT  [DONE]
-----------------------------
Paths: /admin/products, /admin/products/new,
       /admin/products/[id]/edit

- Product list with image, category, price, stock and status.
- Search, category filter and visibility filter.
- Add product form with:
  - name, category, finish and description
  - price in DZD and stock quantity
  - photo upload from the device (resized and compressed automatically)
  - optional image URL or background colour
  - "New" and "On sale" badges
  - live preview before saving
- Create a new category from the product form without leaving the page.
  New categories appear in the admin filter, shop filter and home cards.
- Edit any product:
  - change price, stock and description
  - change name, category, finish, image and badges
- Hide or show a product:
  - hidden products disappear from the storefront
  - demo products are hidden instead of being deleted
- Delete products added from the admin panel, with a confirmation prompt.
- Low-stock and out-of-stock labels.
- Admin changes are shared with the storefront through one catalogue store.

Why it helps:
- The owner can manage the catalogue without editing code.
- The live preview reduces mistakes before saving.
- Hiding is safer than deleting when a product is temporarily unavailable.

Note:
- Changes, categories and uploaded images are saved in the browser
  (localStorage) until the backend is connected. They survive reloads in the
  same browser.


5. ORDER PHONE NUMBER  [DONE]
-----------------------------
Paths: /admin/orders, /admin/orders/[id] and the dashboard table

- Every order includes a phone number.
- The number is shown in the orders table, order details and dashboard.
- It is a clickable tel: link, so the owner can call the customer directly.
- Order search also matches phone numbers.
- Checkout collects and validates the phone number.

Why it helps:
- Faster delivery confirmation.
- Fewer failed deliveries because the customer can be contacted.
- Useful for cash-on-delivery orders.


6. EDITABLE ORDER STATUS AND HISTORY  [DONE]
--------------------------------------------
Paths: /admin/orders and /admin/orders/[id]

- Each order has a status dropdown:
  Pending / Shipped / Delivered / Cancelled.
- Status can be changed from the orders table or the details page.
- Every change is appended to the order's status history with a timestamp.
- Dashboard totals and status breakdown update automatically.
- Internal notes can be added and saved for each order.

Why it helps:
- The owner can track fulfilment without extra tools.
- Pending orders are easy to identify.
- The team can see what happened and when.


7. ORDER DETAILS PAGE  [DONE]
-----------------------------
Path: /admin/orders/[id]

- Full customer information: name, email, phone.
- Delivery address, city and postal code.
- Item list with quantity, unit price and line total.
- Subtotal, discount and final total.
- Payment method.
- Status history timeline.
- Internal notes for the team.
- "Print order" uses the browser print dialog.
- "Export JSON" downloads the complete order.

Why it helps:
- The owner has all order information on one page.
- Printing is useful for packing and delivery.
- Exporting makes it easy to share or archive an order until the backend
  is connected.


8. BETTER STOCK FEEDBACK  [DONE]
--------------------------------
- Product cards show "Out of stock" when stock reaches 0.
- Product cards show "Only X left" when stock is low.
- Product detail page:
  - disables "Add to cart" when out of stock
  - shows the remaining quantity for low stock
  - limits the quantity selector to the available stock
- Cart and checkout warn when the requested quantity is no longer available.
- Placing an order decreases stock automatically.
- The shop "In stock" filter uses the live stock level.

Why it helps:
- Customers do not try to buy unavailable products.
- Low-stock warnings create urgency without being misleading.
- Stock stays accurate after each demo order.


9. LOADING AND ERROR STATES  [DONE]
-----------------------------------
- Product grid skeleton cards while products load.
- Admin table skeleton while orders or products load.
- Friendly error message with a "Try again" button.
- Clear message when the backend or catalogue cannot be reached.
- Empty states for no products, no orders and no search results.

Why it helps:
- The page feels responsive instead of blank.
- Customers and admins know what happened and what to do next.
- The same UI is ready for a real API.


10. CHECKOUT VALIDATION AND CONFIRMATION  [DONE]
------------------------------------------------
- Required shipping fields with inline error messages.
- Email format validation.
- Algerian mobile phone validation (05, 06, 07, +213 and 00213).
- 5-digit postal code validation.
- "Place order" stays disabled until every required field is valid.
- Clear order confirmation screen after placing the order.
- Confirmation shows: order number, total, delivery address, phone,
  email and payment method.
- Confirmation explains the next step for cash on delivery or card payment.
- "Continue shopping" and "Back to home" links.
- The cart is cleared after the order is placed.
- The new order is saved in the browser and appears in /admin/orders.

Why it helps:
- Customers know exactly what is wrong before they submit.
- Fewer failed orders because of a missing phone number or address.
- The confirmation screen gives clear reassurance after purchase.
- The owner can find the order in the admin panel immediately.

Note:
- Orders are not saved to a database yet. They are stored in this browser
  until the backend is connected.


11. ADMIN-ADDED PRODUCTS ON THE STOREFRONT  [DONE]
--------------------------------------------------
- The storefront and admin panel now share one catalogue.
- Products added in /admin/products/new appear in /shop.
- Their detail pages work at /product/[id].
- Edited prices, descriptions and images appear on the storefront.
- Hidden products are removed from the shop and product pages.

Why it helps:
- The owner can test a complete product flow without editing code.
- Customers see the same catalogue that the admin manages.


12. EXISTING STOREFRONT FEATURES THAT SUPPORT UX  [DONE]
--------------------------------------------------------
- Product search with live filtering.
- Category, price, stock and sale filters.
- Sorting by price and name.
- Product detail page with quantity selector and finish colours.
- Persistent cart with quantity controls and order summary.
- Promo code field in the cart.
- Payment choice: cash on delivery, CIB and EDAHABIA.
- Arabic/English switch with right-to-left layout support.
- Mobile-friendly layouts.
- Empty-cart and no-results states.

Why it helps:
- Customers can find products quickly.
- The cart and checkout flow stay understandable.
- The site works for both English and Arabic speakers.

LIMITATIONS OF THE CURRENT DEMO
-------------------------------
- There is no backend or database yet.
- Admin changes, stock, categories and orders are stored in the browser.
- Uploaded product photos are compressed in the browser and stored there too.
- Different browsers or devices do not share data.
- /admin is not protected by a login.
- Print output depends on the browser print settings.
